About me

Who is Riad Adouane?

A true son of the Ras El Aioun district and the Rahbat countryside, in every sense of the word. I was born here, grew up, studied and worked here. I hold a Master's degree in Law and today work as an executive at a national company.

I am one of you: I know the roads, the water supply and the street lighting in every neighbourhood, because I have lived with them just like you. And I continue to work for a more beautiful, better Rahbat, with fast and sustainable development.

My legal background matters because the Communal Assembly approves the budget and the projects. Knowing the law means reading files carefully before signing, defending the commune's rights before the administrations, and protecting public funds. It also means explaining to citizens their rights and how to follow up on their files, so they are no longer sent from office to office.

“Too young, no experience?”A fair question. Experience matters, which is why I am on a list with experienced members from whom I learn every day. But young people bring what the commune needs today: time, energy, and knowledge of modern administration and digitalisation. And the youth of Rahbat have the right to a voice of their own inside the Assembly.

My vision

Rahbat at its best, socially and economically

My vision is the dream of every citizen of Rahbat: that the coming term ends with Rahbat at its best. My goal is not to sit in a chair at the town hall. That seat is a place of work, entrusted to me by the citizens of Rahbat to pursue clear objectives — with citizens as genuine partners, not spectators.

Public services

Citizens should receive the services they are entitled to — easily and close to home, in the outlying hamlets as well as in the town centre.

Jobs for our young people

We will put our energy into creating job opportunities and bringing private investors to our commune, which has real potential for investment across many sectors.

  1. Employment and investmentWe will reach out to the private sector to invest in Rahbat, because wealth created here benefits everyone — young people above all.
  2. CommerceRevitalising local trade through practical initiatives that protect it from stagnation and help it grow.
  3. AgricultureThe area is rich in development opportunities that need practical plans, usable farm tracks, and support for farmers with subsidy and insurance applications.
  4. Mountain tourismIts success depends on the plans of local authorities, and we will push for it to be built into local development programmes. I have already discussed initiatives with private investors interested in the sector.
  5. Higher educationInitiatives in partnership with the communes of the Ras El Aioun district to bring some university services to the area.
  6. DigitalisationWe will not settle for simply receiving e-services: we want the IT engineers and technicians of our region to create wealth with their skills.
  7. Artisans and craftspeopleAcross every kind of service and product, we want to see them thrive over the next five years, through initiatives we will present to the local authorities so they receive support at district, provincial and national level.

My commitments

Three commitments I take responsibility for

Commitment 1

An open door every week

A weekly reception for citizens, where every request is recorded and receives an answer.

Commitment 2

Ending isolation

A clear priority list of roads and tracks to the outlying hamlets, announced publicly and followed up in full view.

Commitment 3

Transparency

The cost, timeline and progress of every municipal project will be published, so that everyone can hold us to account.

!

Why don't I promise housing and jobs?

Because they are not decided by the Communal Assembly alone, and someone who knows the law does not promise what is not in their power. I promise what falls within the Assembly's remit: the budget, local projects, and following up your files with the administrations. And I am not asking for blind trust: every six months there will be an open meeting in each area, where I report what has been achieved, what has not, and why.

Initiatives and messages

From idea to initiative

A feasibility study for mountain tourism

We will ask the provincial authorities for a feasibility study of tourism projects in the area, mountain tourism in particular. With its mild climate and beautiful landscapes, our commune could become a summer destination for families from the southern provinces, opening up opportunities for investment and jobs.

Rural women as partners in development

We are working to put in place what is needed for an initiative dedicated to rural women, which we will present to everyone committed to involving rural women and women artisans in local development.

“Don't let others decide in your place. Your vote on 28 November is the strongest message you can send. And the doors of the Assembly will be open to you: to associations, to sport, to small businesses, and to every idea that serves the commune.”
“Every day you carry the burdens of the home, the children, the water and the school — and your vote makes the difference. Supporting rural women and women artisans with their subsidy applications is part of our programme.”
“Agriculture is the heart of Rahbat. We want usable farm tracks, support for farmers with subsidy and insurance applications, and a voice for them inside the Assembly that defends them before the relevant services. Development must reach the hamlets just as it reaches the town centre.”

Responsibility

Ready for responsibility, with humility

“I do not fear responsibility, but I fear God in the responsibility entrusted to me.”

Office in the commune is a duty, not an honour. It requires knowledge of the law, dedication, closeness to people, and teamwork. A commune is not run by one person but by a united team — which is why I chose the National Liberation Front list: it brings together experience and youth, men and women from every part of Rahbat.

The President of the People's Communal Assembly is elected by the Assembly's elected members. The stronger List No. 1 is, and the more marks there are next to Riad Adouane's name, the stronger his position on the Assembly to serve you.
